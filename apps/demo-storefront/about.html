About this demonstration

This site is a sample catalogue for the Guided Commerce browser extension. The products and descriptions are illustrative. Prices, stock, suitability, delivery and checkout are not connected to a live merchant.

Open the extension from the browser toolbar, choose Analyze this page, then visit the shop page and add it to the guide. The extension reads only pages you choose on this shop.
