ABOUT THIS DEMO

A small shop with room to explore.

This sample storefront exists to give the extension real product pages to read. Its catalogue and copy are illustrative; there is no live stock or checkout.

What the guide can know: details present on the pages you choose to analyze. What it cannot know: current availability, delivery, suitability or anything the merchant has not published.
